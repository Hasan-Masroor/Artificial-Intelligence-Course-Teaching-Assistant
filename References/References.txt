📌 Fundamentals of AI — References

Russell, Stuart J., and Peter Norvig.
Artificial Intelligence: A Modern Approach.
4th Global Edition.

A comprehensive reference for the fundamental concepts and major approaches
in artificial intelligence. The book covers topics including intelligent
agents, problem-solving, search, constraint satisfaction, game playing,
probabilistic reasoning, machine learning, and reinforcement learning.

Recommended as a primary reference for the Fundamentals of Artificial
Intelligence course and for further study of its core topics.

📝 Note:
The topics presented in the course slides do not necessarily follow the
same order as they are presented in the book.
